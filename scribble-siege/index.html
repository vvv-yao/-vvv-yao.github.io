How to play

Monsters come from every side. Draw your army, hold the castle, and save up for an ultimate.

    Try it here

    Pick a tower and draw it on the page. You have ten seconds from your first stroke. Then see what grade it gets.

    A tiny web version of the drawing judge, using stroke matching. The game itself uses an on-device model, and the score here doesn't count anywhere.

    10.0 Draw here with your finger or mouse

    Ink = element

    Your ink color is your element. Every element also has its own shape, so you can tell them apart without relying on color.

    RedFire burns
    BlueIce slows and freezes
    YellowLightning chains
    GreenPoison spreads
    BlackPlain ink, plain power
