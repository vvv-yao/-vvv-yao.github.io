Squawk Rush

A voice-controlled arcade climber. There are no buttons: your voice moves the bird, and the front camera puts your face behind the game. Every run ends with a highlight clip worth sending to a friend.

Humand your bird walks.
Shoutand it jumps.
Screamand it flies higher.

Try it right here

A tiny taste of the game. Turn on your microphone and shout: the louder you are, the higher the bird jumps. No microphone? Hold the stage and let go, the longer you hold, the bigger the jump.

Your audio stays on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Seven ways to play

Climb alone, race a ghost, or pass one phone around the room.

Daily Challenge

The whole world climbs the same tower every day. Ranked runs are replayed on the server, so scores can't be faked.

Ghost Battle

Race a replay of the player just above you. The ghost waits for you and talks trash.

Party

One phone, up to six friends, and a lot of noise.

Mute bombs

Spikes, meteors and fire arrows knock you around. Land on a mute bomb and the only way out is silence.

Glide & Endless Run

Glide between the pipes as the speed builds, or dash through an endless run with power-ups.

Free Climb at your own pace Scale Mode sing the right note to jump

Built for Seeker

Play without a wallet. Connect one when you want your score to count.

Wallet sign-in

Sign in with Seed Vault or Phantom over Mobile Wallet Adapter to join the leaderboard and race ghosts.

A leaderboard of your own

Seeker owners get a Seeker-only leaderboard, and Seeker Genesis Token holders earn login rewards just by playing.

SKR cosmetics

Skins, hats, trails and No Ads, paid in SKR and verified on chain. Your look shows in every mode and in every clip.

Fair by design

Cosmetics never change the rules, and every ranked run is checked by replaying it with the same game code.
